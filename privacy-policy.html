Privacy Policy

Stack & Merge (package ai.stackmerge.app)
Last updated: October 7, 2026
Developer: Ihab Hassan (independent developer)
Contact: ai.wise.au@gmail.com

Stack & Merge is developed and maintained by Ihab Hassan as an independent project. This policy explains what information the Stack & Merge mobile game ("the app") collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. We describe only what the app actually does at the time of writing.

1. Summary

  • You do not create an account with an email address or password. The app signs you in anonymously and gives you a random player ID.
  • Your progress (coins, best score, power-up counts, daily streak) is stored on your device and in the cloud under that player ID.
  • Your score, optional display name and country (from your device's region setting) are public on the leaderboards.
  • The app shows ads through Google AdMob, which may use your advertising ID and device information.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play; we never see your payment details.
  • Reminder notifications are created on your device and nothing about them is sent to us.
  • We do not sell personal information.

2. Information we collect

What we do not collect: your name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise or approximate location from sensors, photos, microphone or camera data, or health or financial information. The app does not ask for these permissions.

Reporting a display name

Display names are checked against a filter before they are saved, but no filter is perfect. If you see an inappropriate display name on a leaderboard, email ai.wise.au@gmail.com with the subject "Stack & Merge Name Report" and the name you saw; we will remove it. Reports are handled by email.

Information stored only on your device

Your settings (sound, music, vibration, difficulty), your best score, wallet and streak counters, whether you have seen certain prompts, and your choice about reminder notifications are stored locally with the Android preferences system. Reminder notifications are scheduled by your device; the app does not send notification content, or the fact that you enabled notifications, to our servers, and it does not use a push-notification service.

3. How we use information

  • To run the game: save and restore progress, grant daily rewards and purchases, keep leaderboards.
  • To show ads and measure them (through Google), which funds the free game.
  • To prevent cheating and abuse of the leaderboards and wallet.
  • To answer your questions and requests, including deletion requests.

We do not use your information for profiling beyond what advertising partners do under the settings in section 5, and we do not make automated decisions that have legal effects on you.

4. Who we share information with

We do not sell personal information. Information is processed by these providers acting for us or on their own terms:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore): stores the anonymous account and the data in section 2. firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads: ads. policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • Google Play (Billing and the Play Store): purchases. policies.google.com/privacy
  • Other players: your leaderboard entry is public, as described above.
  • Authorities or advisers: if the law requires it, or to protect our rights, we may disclose information to the extent necessary.

5. Advertising and your choices

The app shows two kinds of ads served by Google AdMob: rewarded videos you choose to watch (for example to refill a power-up or continue after a game over) and short ads between some games. AdMob may collect and use the Android advertising ID, IP address, device information and ad interactions to deliver and measure ads, which can include personalised ads.

  • Ads personalisation: on your device go to Settings > Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads) to reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalisation.
  • Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland: where the law requires it, the app asks for your consent before any ad is requested, using Google's consent form (the Google User Messaging Platform). You can review or change your choice at any time under Settings > Your data > Privacy options, which appears wherever the consent form applies. Until you have made a choice where consent is required, the app shows no ads.
  • The app also works without personalised ads; non-personalised ads are still shown.

6. Children

Stack & Merge is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us at ai.wise.au@gmail.com and we will promptly delete it.

7. How long we keep information

  • Your progress record and leaderboard entry are kept for as long as the anonymous account exists, until you delete them (in the app or by email, see section 8).
  • Data stored only on your device is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its storage.
  • Google keeps advertising, billing and Firebase data under its own policies.

8. Deleting your data and your rights

In the app: open Settings > Your data > Delete my data and confirm. This immediately deletes your progress record and your leaderboard entry from our servers, removes your anonymous account, erases everything the app stored on your device and starts the game over as if freshly installed. It cannot be undone, and purchases are not refunded. Deletion needs an internet connection; if it cannot reach our servers the app tells you and changes nothing, so you can try again.

By email: if you can no longer open the app, email ai.wise.au@gmail.com with the subject "Stack & Merge Data Deletion Request". Include your player ID (shown under Settings > Your data > Player ID; tapping it copies the full ID) so that we can find your records. If you no longer have the ID, include your display name, country and best score, and we may ask for additional proof of ownership. We will process your request within 30 days and confirm by email once it is complete. A short step-by-step page is at data-deletion.html.

Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, UK, Switzerland, California or Brazil) you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal information, to object to or restrict certain processing, to withdraw consent, and to lodge a complaint with your data-protection authority. Email us to exercise these rights. Because the app has no email address or name tied to your player ID, we can only act on a request if we can reasonably identify your record.

Deleting your data also removes your public leaderboard entry. Reinstalling the app or clearing its data on your device normally creates a new anonymous player ID with a new empty record; the old record is not deleted by this (use the steps above first if you want it gone).

9. Security and international transfers

Data sent between the app and Firebase, AdMob and Google Play is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Access to the progress record is restricted by security rules so that only the signed-in player ID can read or change it; the leaderboard record is publicly readable by design. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Google processes data in many countries, including the United States, under its own safeguards such as standard contractual clauses. The Firestore database is located in the United States (nam5).

10. Legal bases (EEA / UK users)

  • Providing the game and your progress: performance of a contract (our terms of use of the game).
  • Leaderboards (public display name, country and score): our legitimate interest in running a competitive game, and your choice of display name.
  • Personalised advertising: your consent where required.
  • Preventing cheating and abuse: our legitimate interests.
